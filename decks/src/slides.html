Roadmap

Prove it on one brand, then scale it

Phase 1
MVP

Web agent on Brightcurrent that reads photos and care labels, remembers the customer, and opens cases. Voice, returns and payments are out of scope.

Phase 2
Expand

Softly and Fresh Spin, Slack for care agents and mobile, once Phase 1 hits its resolution and satisfaction targets.

Phase 3
Vision

12 months and beyond: proactive care reminders, voice, and one assistant across every Hallworth & Pierce brand.

Why this sequence? Value shows up fastest where photos and repeat contacts are concentrated. The same topics and data then carry over to every other brand.
