  2. Start the email backlog agent so the 3,850 open cases get worked down.
  3. Hold us to the goal at the 90 day review.

Why an email backlog agent

Email is where customers wait: 3,850 open cases and a 4.2 day first reply. The email agent reads every message in the queue, resolves tier 1 questions, drafts replies for the care team to approve, and sends safety issues to a person first. That is how backlog time gets cut in half.
