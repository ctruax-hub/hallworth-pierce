Deployment plan

From sandbox to every customer, one gate at a time

What moves

The agent, its subagents, actions, prompt templates and flows. Data 360 streams, DLO to DMO mappings and the search index.

Exit criteria

Unit tests pass in Testing Center. Every change is peer reviewed and in version control.

Sign off

Lead Forward Deployed Engineer.

What moves

The same release through the DevOps Center pipeline, plus the messaging channel and the embedded chat on a staging site.

Exit criteria

The full test plan passes at 95% or better, with zero safety failures. Security review is complete.

Sign off

Customer care leadership and IT security.

What moves

Live on Brightcurrent for 10% of web visitors, with handoff to a person always on.

Exit criteria

Two weeks with 25% or more of cases resolved, satisfaction at or above today, and no safety incidents.

Sign off

Executive sponsor, at the go or no go meeting.

What moves

All Brightcurrent visitors, around the clock. Softly and Fresh Spin follow using the same release path.

Exit criteria

Targets held for 30 days, then hypercare closes and the agent moves to business as usual support.

Sign off

Project steering committee.

Rollback

Every release is versioned. If anything goes wrong, we deactivate the agent version and send chats to the care team in minutes.
